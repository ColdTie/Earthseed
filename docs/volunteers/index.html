TRIP Volunteer Driver Program Ongoing — tap for details

TRIP (Transportation Reimbursement and Information Project) connects volunteer drivers with seniors and people with disabilities who can no longer drive. You drive them to doctor appointments, pharmacies, grocery stores, and other errands. The county reimburses them 45 cents per mile, and they pay you back. No training required. You drive on your own schedule.

What you do
Drive a senior or disabled neighbor to appointments and everyday errands. You set your own availability. There is no minimum hours requirement.
Schedule
Flexible. You commit only to drives you agree to take. Ongoing program, not event-based.
Mileage
You get reimbursed. The rider receives a monthly check from the county to pay you back at 45¢/mile. This is not a paid job, but it is not out of pocket either.
Requirements
  • Valid California driver's license
  • Current vehicle registration and insurance
  • No special training or certification needed
Service area
All of Riverside County, including Menifee.
How to sign up
Call Independent Living Partnership at (951) 653-0740. You are named as the rider's volunteer driver when they enroll. Enrollment for riders takes about a month, so start the conversation early.

This is the program to call if you want to do something like Meals on Wheels. The work is real and the need in Menifee is real.

Menifee Valley Community Cupboard Ongoing — tap for details

The Cupboard feeds 900+ Menifee families and runs a thrift store to fund that work. They are actively looking for volunteers and posted a call for help as recently as April 2025.

What you do
Two tracks: (1) Food pantry — collect and sort donated food items, help with distribution. (2) Thrift store — cashiering, sorting, shelving, merchandising clothing and goods.
Schedule
Mon–Fri during operating hours (10 AM–2 PM). Flexible scheduling; ongoing commitment preferred but not required.
Requirements
18+ to volunteer independently. Under 18 must be accompanied by an adult. High school and college students welcome — service hours available.
Address
26808 Cherry Hills Blvd, Menifee 92586
How to sign up
Visit mvcupboard.org/volunteer or call (951) 301-4414 to ask about current needs.

Project T.O.U.C.H. Flexible — tap for details

Project T.O.U.C.H. provides shared housing and wraparound support for 275+ people facing homelessness in Southwest Riverside County — seniors, veterans, single parents, and people with disabilities. Over 400 volunteers keep this running.

What you do
Mentoring residents, helping at fundraising events, facility clean-up and organization days. The organization is also building a new Life Restoration Center in Wildomar, so construction-adjacent help may be available.
Schedule
Mix of ongoing mentoring and one-time events. Flexible to your availability.
Requirements
Varies by role. Call or check their website for current openings.
Address
30141 Antelope Rd, Suite D-103, Menifee 92584
How to sign up
Visit projecttouchonline.com/volunteer

THINK Together — After-School Tutoring Ongoing — tap for details

THINK Together runs after-school programs in Menifee and Perris schools, helping students who need extra support. Volunteers help with tutoring, mentoring, and program activities.

What you do
Work with students in after-school programs at Menifee and Perris schools. Tutoring, reading support, mentoring.
Schedule
After school hours, typically 2–6 PM on school days. Ongoing during the school year.
Requirements
Adult volunteers coming to campus regularly must complete an application. Background check required for work with minors.
How to sign up
Visit thinktogether.org/school-volunteer or the JustServe listing for Menifee/Perris schools.

Menifee Better Together City events — tap for details

The city of Menifee's community volunteer program. Events throughout the year — park cleanups, beautification projects, community outreach. Good for one-time participation if you want to try volunteering without a recurring commitment.

What you do
Beautification projects, community event setup, outreach support. Varies by event.
Schedule
Periodic events; not a weekly commitment. Check cityofmenifee.us for upcoming events.
Requirements
Open to all ages. Some events may require parental permission for minors.
How to sign up
Check cityofmenifee.us or call (951) 672-6777 for the Parks & Community Services department.

Riverside County RSVP / AmeriCorps Seniors Ongoing — tap for details

The Retired and Senior Volunteer Program (RSVP), run through the Riverside County Office on Aging, places volunteers age 55+ in meaningful community roles. This is a federal AmeriCorps Seniors program — it is well-organized and matches volunteers to placements that fit their interests and skills.

Who it is for
Adults 55 and older. No upper age limit.
What you do
Matched to a volunteer role based on your interests — could include senior services, community outreach, nutrition programs, and more. Special events also available.
Schedule
Flexible, matched to your availability. Ongoing.
Requirements
Age 55+. No specific qualifications required for most roles.
How to sign up
Call the Riverside County Office on Aging HelpLink at (877) 932-4100 and ask about RSVP volunteer placements.
(877) 932-4100 rcaging.org
