The senior center

Menifee's senior center — meals, activities, and on-site assistance

Kay Ceniceros Senior Center — tap for details

The Kay Ceniceros Senior Center is the main hub for seniors in Menifee. It offers hot lunches on weekdays, exercise and enrichment classes, and a place to connect with neighbors. Community Action Partnership also operates there, so utility assistance appointments are available on-site.

Lunch
Hot meal served Monday–Thursday, 11:30 AM–12:30 PM. Reservation required 24 hours in advance. Suggested donation: $3 for age 60+, $7 for under 60. Call (951) 679-0119 to reserve.
Who can come
Adults 60 and older (or married to someone 60+). Activities and classes open to members; call to ask about membership.
Utility assistance
Community Action Partnership (CAP) is available on-site to help with LIHEAP energy bill assistance and other programs. Call the center to ask about CAP appointment availability.
Activities
Exercise classes, enrichment classes, recreation and socialization programs. Call for the current schedule.

For seniors who cannot leave home

YANA Welfare Check Program — tap for details

The Menifee Police Department runs a free program called "You Are Not Alone" (YANA). Volunteer callers from the police department check in by phone at a prearranged time each day. If the senior does not answer, the program contacts their emergency contact and, if needed, sends someone to the home.

Who it is for
Older and disabled adults who live alone in the City of Menifee.
What happens
A volunteer calls at the time you agree to. If there is no answer, your emergency contact is notified. If needed, someone comes to the home.
Requirements
  • Able to answer the phone
  • One emergency contact (family, friend, or neighbor)
Cost
Free.
How to enroll
Email the YANA Program Coordinator at DG_PD_Volunteers@menifeepolice.org to get a registration form, or mail a completed form to:
Menifee Police Department, ATTN: YANA
29714 Haun Rd, Unit A
Menifee, CA 92586
Referrals
Family members and friends who know someone who would benefit can make a referral by emailing the coordinator or sending a letter to the address above. You do not have to wait for the senior to ask.

This is one of the most underused programs in Menifee. If you know a senior who lives alone and would benefit from a daily check-in call, a referral from you is all it takes to get them enrolled.

Riverside County Home Delivered Meals — tap for details

The Riverside County Office on Aging delivers hot or frozen meals to homebound seniors throughout the county, including Menifee. The program is not widely known. If you or someone you know cannot get to a food pantry or the senior center, this is the first call to make.

Who can use it
Adults age 60 or older, or adults with a disability, who are homebound and have difficulty preparing their own meals. No income requirement. Covers all of Riverside County including Menifee.
What they provide
Hot or frozen meals delivered to your home. An emergency two-week pantry supply is also available.
How to start
Call the HelpLink line. Intake is done by phone — no office visit required. After enrollment, the delivery schedule is arranged.
Call hours
Monday–Friday, 9 AM–4 PM
(877) 932-4100

Getting to appointments

TRIP Volunteer Driver Program — tap for details

The Transportation Reimbursement and Information Project connects seniors and people with disabilities who can no longer drive with volunteer drivers. You get a ride to doctor appointments, pharmacies, grocery stores, and other errands. The county reimburses you 45 cents per mile so you can pay the driver back. The driver sets their own schedule; there are no fixed routes.

Who it is for
Seniors and adults with disabilities who cannot drive themselves. Serves all of Riverside County including Menifee.
Cost to rider
You receive a monthly mileage reimbursement check from the county (45¢/mile) and use it to pay your driver. This is not a taxi — you arrange rides in advance with a volunteer who agrees to help you.
Enrollment time
Enrollment takes approximately one month. Call early, before you are in urgent need.
How to enroll
Call Independent Living Partnership at (951) 653-0740.

More detail on the TRIP program, including how to sign up as a volunteer driver, is on the volunteer opportunities page.

(951) 653-0740

If you are caring for a senior

Riverside County Family Caregiver Support Program — tap for details

The Riverside County Office on Aging runs a free program for family members who care for a senior. Caregiver burnout is real and it is rarely talked about. This program offers education, peer support, and limited respite care so the caregiver can take a break.

Care Pathways workshop
A free 11-week workshop series covering dementia, Alzheimer's, caregiver burnout, legal issues, and communication. Open to caregivers of adults 60 and older.
Support groups
Monthly peer support groups at locations throughout the county. A place to talk with other people in the same situation.
Respite care
Arrangements for a short break for the caregiver, with care provided for their family member in the interim. Subject to available funding; ask when you call.
Who can use it
Family caregivers of adults age 60 and older. No income requirement.
Cost
Free.
How to reach them
Call the HelpLink line and ask about the Family Caregiver Support Program. They will connect you to the right person.
(877) 932-4100 rcaging.org

Utility bills and financial help

LIHEAP Energy Assistance — tap for details

The Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP) helps low-income households pay utility bills. In Riverside County it is run by Community Action Partnership (CAP). Seniors age 60 and older receive priority. If you are struggling with an electric or gas bill, this is the program to call.

Who qualifies
Households at or below 150% of the Federal Poverty Level, or 60% of the California state median income (whichever is higher). Seniors 60+, people with disabilities, and households with children under 6 receive priority.
What it covers
Utility bill payment assistance and weatherization services (help making your home more energy-efficient to lower future bills).
How to apply
Call (951) 955-4900. A phone pre-screen is required before scheduling an appointment. You can also ask the Kay Ceniceros Senior Center staff about on-site CAP appointments.
Application
By appointment. Walk-in emergency hours may be available; ask when you call.

SHARE (Sharing Households Assist Riverside's Energy) is a related program for additional utility assistance. Ask about both when you call CAP.

(951) 955-4900 capriverside.org
