Riverside County Home Delivered Meals Seniors & disabled — tap for details

…
How it works
Meals are delivered to your home. Call to start the intake process. No office visit required to enroll.
Hours to call
Monday9 AM – 4 PM
Tuesday9 AM – 4 PM
Wednesday9 AM – 4 PM
Thursday9 AM – 4 PM
Friday9 AM – 4 PM
Who can use it
Adults age 60 or older, or adults with a disability, who are homebound and have difficulty preparing their own meals. Serves all of Riverside County including Menifee. No income requirement.
What they provide
Hot or frozen meals delivered to your door. Emergency two-week pantry supply also available.

This program exists but is not widely known. If you or someone you know cannot get to a food pantry, this is the right call to make first.

(877) 932-4100
