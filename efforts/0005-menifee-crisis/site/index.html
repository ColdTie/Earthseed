⚠ If your SNAP food benefits just went down

The 2025 federal budget law changed how SNAP calculates benefits for households that also receive LIHEAP utility assistance. If you use both programs, your monthly SNAP amount may be lower than it was before July 2025.

  • Call (951) 955-4900 (CAP Riverside) to confirm you are still enrolled in LIHEAP and ask how the change affects your household.
  • Call 211 to ask whether there is emergency food help to bridge the gap while you sort it out.
  • The Menifee Valley Community Cupboard (food resources page) is open Monday through Friday, 10 AM to 2 PM, at 26808 Cherry Hills Blvd.

CAP Riverside — Emergency Services  — tap for details

Utilities Emergency food Weatherization
What they do
Community Action Partnership of Riverside County runs several emergency programs beyond the LIHEAP utility assistance most people know. They can provide emergency utility help for households that do not qualify for LIHEAP or have already used it for the year, emergency food boxes, and weatherization services (insulation, sealing, minor repairs) that reduce ongoing energy costs.
Who qualifies
Low-income households in Riverside County. Eligibility varies by program. Seniors 60 and older are a priority group for most CAP programs. They will assess your situation over the phone.
How to apply
Call first. They pre-screen by phone and set an appointment. Do not just show up without calling. Have your most recent utility bill, proof of income, and ID ready when you call.
Phone
(951) 955-4900  ·  Mon–Fri 8 AM–5 PM

Riverside County DPSS — CalWORKs & General Relief  — tap for details

Cash aid Families with children Adults without children Medi-Cal enrollment
CalWORKs
Cash aid and job services for families with children under 18. Provides monthly cash while helping parents find work. Amount varies by family size and income. Applications through the county Department of Public Social Services.
General Relief
Cash assistance for adults who do not have dependent children and do not qualify for other programs. Smaller amounts than CalWORKs. Intended for short-term crisis situations. Requires application and eligibility review.
How to apply
Visit dpss.rivco.org to apply online, or call 211 to get routed to the nearest DPSS office. The Hemet DPSS office is the closest to Menifee: 700 N State St, Hemet. You can also apply at any DPSS location in the county.
Medi-Cal
DPSS also handles Medi-Cal enrollment. If you lost coverage or need to re-enroll, they can help. The Medi-Cal work requirements added by the 2025 federal budget law start December 31, 2026 — DPSS will be the agency processing those requirements.

DPSS offices are walk-in, but wait times can be long. Calling 211 first to confirm which office to visit and what documents to bring will save time.

Salvation Army Murrieta — Emergency Assistance  — tap for details

Utility help Rent help Emergency food
What they do
The Salvation Army Murrieta Corps provides emergency assistance to residents in Murrieta and the surrounding area including Menifee. Services can include one-time utility bill assistance, emergency food, and in some cases rental assistance. Availability of funds varies by season and donation levels.
How to apply
Call first to ask what is currently available and set an appointment. Walk-in assistance is appointment only. Bring ID, proof of address, and the bill you need help with (utility shutoff notice or lease/eviction notice).

Mental health & personal crisis

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911. If it is a mental health crisis that is not immediately life-threatening, these lines connect you to trained crisis counselors, not emergency dispatch.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
Free, confidential, 24/7. Available in English and Spanish. For suicidal thoughts, mental health crises, or emotional distress. Also available via chat at 988lifeline.org.
988 Call or text, 24/7
Crisis Text Line
Free text-based crisis support, 24/7. For people who cannot make a phone call or prefer to communicate by text. Trained counselors, not an automated system.
Text HOME to 741741 Free text, 24/7
Riverside County Mental Health Crisis Line
24-hour crisis line for Riverside County residents. Connects to a local mental health clinician. Can also arrange a mobile crisis response — a mental health worker comes to you instead of police.

If you might lose your housing

Act before you receive an eviction notice Emergency rental assistance is much easier to get before a formal eviction notice is filed. Once an unlawful detainer (eviction lawsuit) is filed, your options narrow significantly. Call 211 as soon as you know you will miss rent.

Emergency Rental Assistance — Where to Start  — tap for details

211 first
Call or text 211. They maintain the current list of open rental assistance programs in Riverside County. Funds open and close on short notice — 211 knows which ones are currently accepting applications.
Riverside County
Riverside County has run emergency rental assistance programs and may have current funding. Ask 211 or check dpss.rivco.org for current status.
Project T.O.U.C.H.
A homeless prevention program operating in the Menifee area. They work specifically on keeping people housed before a crisis becomes homelessness. Contact through 211, which can route you to their current intake.
What to have ready
Lease or rental agreement. Last month's rent receipt or bank statement showing the payment. Any notice from your landlord. Proof of income. Photo ID. Most programs need all of these to process an application.

Medi-Cal work requirements — December 2026

Deadline: December 31, 2026 If you are on Medi-Cal and between ages 19–65 on the ACA Medicaid expansion, you will need to document 80 hours per month of qualifying activity, or claim an exemption, by December 31, 2026. The risk is not that you do not qualify for an exemption — it is that you do qualify but miss the paperwork deadline.

What the Medi-Cal work requirement means for you  — tap for details

Who it affects
Adults ages 19–65 enrolled in Medi-Cal through the ACA Medicaid expansion, who do not have a documented disability or other exemption. If you were already on Medi-Cal before the ACA expansion, different rules may apply.
What qualifies as 80 hours
Employment, paid or unpaid job training, education toward a degree or certificate, or volunteer work at an approved organization. Hours can be combined — 20 hours of part-time work plus 60 hours of school counts.
Who is exempt (but must file paperwork)
People who are medically fragile, pregnant, caring for a child under 6 or a disabled family member, enrolled in substance use treatment, or living in a county with high unemployment. Exemptions are not automatic — you must document and file them.
What to do now
Call (877) 932-4100 (Riverside County HelpLink) and ask about Medi-Cal work requirements and whether you need to file an exemption. Do this before December 2026. Do not wait for a notice — notices often arrive close to the deadline.
