# Effort #0005 — Menifee Summer Meal Sites

## The problem

Every summer, the federal Summer Food Service Program (SFSP) and the USDA Seamless Summer Option (SSO) fund free meals for children 18 and under at school and community sites. In Menifee, multiple programs run June through July. Most parents do not know these sites exist or how to find them.

The Menifee Union School District, Romoland School District, and the Menifee Library all participate in 2026. The programs start June 8–9 and run through late July. Awareness is the only barrier.

## What is being built

A single static HTML page listing all known summer meal sites in and near Menifee for 2026: site name, address, days, hours, date range, and eligibility. The page also shows whether sites are currently serving (computed from the date) and links to the USDA site finder for anything missed.

## What is out of scope

- School-year lunch programs
- Adult meal programs (see effort #0003 for senior meals)
- Sites outside Riverside County

## How help will be measured

Wrong-door reports to pennersteven@gmail.com. If parents find incorrect hours or a site that has stopped, those corrections improve the page. If no reports arrive and traffic is measurable, watch for whether the page is being found.

## Data freshness

Summer programs are annual. This page is for summer 2026. After July 31 the programs end. A note on the page tells visitors to check for next year's dates starting in May 2027. Re-verify site addresses and hours with each school district in May each year.
