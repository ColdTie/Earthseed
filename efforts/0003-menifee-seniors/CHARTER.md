# Effort #0003: Menifee Senior Resource Guide

## Status: Planned — next after #0002 is live

## The problem

Menifee's population is growing and skewing older. The city has a large 55+ community and a significant number of homebound seniors. The services that exist for them — home-delivered meals, volunteer transportation, caregiver support, utility assistance, welfare checks — are not visible in one place and many residents and their families do not know they exist.

The most concrete gap found so far: there is no Meals on Wheels affiliate serving Menifee. The Riverside County chapter covers Riverside city and nearby areas, not the southwest part of the county. The Riverside County Office on Aging does have a Home Delivered Meals Program that covers all of Riverside County including Menifee, but it is not well-known locally. People who need it do not know to call (877) 932-4100.

This effort builds on the food and volunteer work already done and focuses it on seniors specifically.

## What we are building

A single web page for seniors in Menifee (and the family members and neighbors trying to help them) that covers:

- Home-delivered meal programs (county and any private options)
- Volunteer transportation (TRIP program is the primary option)
- Senior centers and congregate meal sites
- Caregiver support resources
- Utility and financial assistance specific to seniors
- Wellness check programs
- How to request help for a neighbor who may be struggling

## What "helped" means here

Wrong-door reports and "someone I know used this" confirmations. The senior population is less likely to fill out a web form. We may need to think about whether the page alone is enough or whether we also need to get printed cards into the hands of doctors, pharmacies, and grocery store workers who interact with seniors regularly.

The TRIP program sign-up rate among Menifee-area riders and drivers is a concrete secondary metric if ILP will share it.

## Research needed before building v1

1. Are there any senior centers physically in Menifee? (Sun City has a history as a senior community — there may be existing infrastructure.)
2. Does the County Office on Aging have an in-person presence or outreach point near Menifee?
3. Are there congregate meal sites (group meal programs for seniors who can leave home but need support)?
4. What utility assistance programs specifically target seniors in Riverside County?
5. Is there a formal welfare check request process through Menifee PD or the fire department?

## Why this is not yet active

Effort #0001 (food resources) and #0002 (volunteer board) are in progress. The senior guide requires more research than either of those — especially on senior center infrastructure — and doing it halfway would produce a page that misleads people. Better to do it right when there is bandwidth.

The county home-delivered meals program has already been added to the #0001 food page as a stopgap. That is the most urgent piece of information for homebound seniors and it is now findable.

## Connection to effort #0002

Steve (the person running this project) wants to drive seniors to appointments through the TRIP program. If he does that, he will have direct contact with homebound seniors and their families. That is the best possible source of firsthand information about what is missing and what the real friction points are. The #0003 page should be informed by what he learns through that direct contact.
