City of Menifee — 29995 Evans Road, Menifee, CA 92586
Hours Monday – Friday, 8 AM – 5 PM

This is Menifee’s only city-operated senior center. Most programs in this guide either connect to or are available at Kay Ceniceros. If you’re not sure where to start, call the center.

  • Congregate lunch — Mon–Fri at 11:30 AM. Call (951) 679-0119 to reserve a spot 24 hours ahead. Ages 60+.
  • Exercise and fitness — T’ai Chi Chih, Uchikomi Fitness, Taiko Fitness, Heart & Soul Line Dancing and more.
  • Arts, enrichment and games — Quilting, Spanish class, bridge, chess, dominos, ping pong, scrabble and more.
  • LIHEAP utility assistance — Community Action Agency rep on-site the 2nd Monday of each month, 12:30–3:30 PM. Come in person for help with electric, gas, and water bills.
  • Cooling center — Open to anyone on days forecast to reach 97°F or above. No registration needed.

Meals at home
Riverside County Home Delivered Meals
Riverside County Office on Aging — county-wide, including Menifee
Who Adults 60+ or disabled adults who are homebound and cannot prepare their own meals
What Hot or frozen meals delivered to your home. No pickup location.
Cost Free. No income requirement.
How to start Call Senior HelpLink to begin intake. No office visit required.
Call hours Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 4 PM
Note
There is no Meals on Wheels affiliate in Menifee. This county program is the equivalent for residents here. Many people who qualify don’t know to call. If you know a homebound senior who needs meals, share this number.

Getting around
TRIP Volunteer Driver Program
Transportation Reimbursement and Information Project — Independent Living Partnership
How it works A volunteer driver — a neighbor, friend, or program volunteer — takes you to appointments and errands. The county reimburses you 45¢ per mile, which you pay the driver.
Schedule Any day, any time, arranged directly between rider and driver
Cost to rider The mileage reimbursement covers the driver. No other cost.
To sign up Call (951) 653-0740

Want to volunteer as a driver? No set schedule or training required. Call the same number to sign up.

Seniors and disabled adults Flexible

Safety and welfare checks
YANA: You Are Not Alone
Menifee Police Department Volunteer Program
What it is Menifee PD volunteers call enrolled seniors at a prearranged time. If the call goes unanswered, they contact your emergency contacts and, if needed, do a home visit.
Who it’s for Older adults and disabled residents living alone in the City of Menifee
What you need Must be able to answer a phone. Must provide at least one emergency contact.
Cost Free
To enroll Email DG_PD_Volunteers@menifeepolice.org or mail a form to the address below
Mail to Menifee PD, ATTN: YANA
29714 Haun Rd, Unit A, Menifee, CA 92586
Referring a neighbor or family member
A friend, neighbor, or family member can refer someone who would benefit from this program — the person does not have to self-enroll.

Support for family caregivers
Inland Caregiver Resource Center (ICRC)
Serving Riverside and San Bernardino Counties, including Menifee
Who it’s for Family caregivers of adults 60+ who need help with two or more daily tasks, or caregivers of adults with dementia, stroke, Parkinson’s, or other brain conditions
  • Family consultation — One-on-one help planning care
  • Case management — Ongoing support navigating services
  • Caregiver support groups — Connect with others in similar situations
  • Short-term counseling — Up to 6 sessions
  • Respite care — In-home help, adult day care, or overnight placement so you can take a break
  • Education — On dementia, burnout, legal issues, and planning ahead
Cost Free to low-cost. Call to ask.
Caregivers Dementia and brain conditions Riverside County

Financial and emergency help
LIHEAP Utility Assistance
Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program — Community Action Partnership
What it covers Help paying electric, gas, and water bills. Heating and cooling equipment repair in some cases.
At Kay Ceniceros A rep is on-site the 2nd Monday of each month, 12:30–3:30 PM. Just show up. Bring a recent utility bill, proof of income, and ID.
By phone (951) 955-4900 (Hemet Service Center)
or (877) 932-4100 (Senior HelpLink)
Eligibility Income limits apply (generally 60% of California median income or below). Call to confirm.
Utility bills Income-based Walk-in available
Smiles 4 Seniors
Emergency medical funding — Riverside County
What it covers One-time emergency funding for medical needs not covered by Medicare or Medi-Cal: medications, medical supplies, hearing aids, glasses, dental care, and other medical necessities.
Who qualifies Age 65+, income under $15,000/year (single) or $30,000 (combined). Proof of income required.
Important One-time use only per person.
Ages 65+ Low income One-time use Medical costs

Senior Minor Home Rehabilitation Grant
City of Menifee — Up to $15,000 for home repairs
What it covers Accessibility improvements, safety repairs (roof, electrical, plumbing, windows), and code violations for qualifying homeowners.
Who qualifies Very low-income seniors or disabled homeowners in Menifee who have owned and lived in the home for at least 1 year.
Currently closed
As of 2026, this program is not accepting new applications due to high demand. The waitlist extends approximately 5 years. Check cityofmenifee.us for updates on reopening.
