Getting Around

TRIP Volunteer Driver Program — tap for details

Call to arrange
What it is
A volunteer driver program run by Independent Living Partnership. Volunteer drivers take seniors and adults with disabilities to medical appointments and errands. Riders are reimbursed 45 cents per mile to pay their driver back.
Who can use it
Seniors and adults with disabilities in and around Menifee who need help getting to appointments or running errands. There is no income test.
Cost
County funding reimburses riders 45 cents per mile. You pay your driver directly with that reimbursement. The amount covers most local trips.
How to enroll
Call Independent Living Partnership to sign up as a rider. They will match you with a volunteer driver for your area.

There is no Meals on Wheels delivery service based in Menifee, but TRIP is the real alternative: a volunteer will drive you to pick up food or take you to a doctor. The county pays for the mileage.

(951) 653-0740

Help at Home

In-Home Supportive Services (IHSS) — tap for details

Apply online or by phone
What it is
A Riverside County program that pays a caregiver — including a family member — to help you with household tasks and personal care so you can remain safely in your own home.
What is covered
  • Meal preparation and clean-up
  • Housecleaning and laundry
  • Grocery shopping and errands
  • Bathing, grooming, and personal care
  • Help taking medications as directed
  • Accompanying you to medical appointments
Who qualifies
Adults who are Medi-Cal eligible and have a physical or mental disability expected to last 12 months or longer — including many seniors. The county sends a social worker to assess your needs.
How to apply
Apply online at riversideihss.org or call the number below. The process starts with a phone screening, then an in-home assessment.

If you are already on Medi-Cal, you may qualify without knowing it. The application is free and there is no cost for approved services.

(888) 960-4477 Apply online

If You Live Alone

YANA Program — You Are Not Alone — tap for details

Free enrollment
What it is
A free program run by Menifee Police Department volunteers. Volunteers make regular phone calls to check on seniors living alone. If no answer and there is concern, a volunteer will do a home visit.
Purpose
Peace of mind for seniors and their families. Not a medical service. If there is a medical emergency, call 911. YANA is the regular check-in between emergencies.
Who can join
Any senior or adult with a disability living in Menifee who is alone and wants regular contact. You must be able to answer the phone and provide one emergency contact name.
Cost
Free.
How to enroll
Email the YANA coordinator or mail your completed registration form to the address below. For help filling out the form, email or write to the same address.
Contact
Email: DG_PD_Volunteers@menifeepolice.org
Mail: Menifee Police Department, ATTN: YANA, 29714 Haun Rd Unit A, Menifee 92586

This program also gives families who live out of the area confidence that someone is checking in on a parent or older relative living alone in Menifee.

For Caregivers

Family Caregiver Support Program — tap for details

Riverside County
What it is
A program through the Riverside County Office on Aging for family members who are caring for an older relative. Provides education, respite care, and support groups.
Care Pathways workshops
A free 11-week workshop series for caregivers. Topics include dementia and Alzheimer's care, avoiding caregiver burnout, legal issues, and communication. Offered throughout the county.
Respite care
Temporary relief for caregivers — someone comes in to care for your family member so you can rest or take care of other obligations. Availability depends on funding; call to ask what is currently available.
Support groups
Monthly groups throughout Riverside County where caregivers meet others in similar situations, with a trained facilitator. Call to find the group nearest to Menifee.
How to connect
Call the HelpLink line to be connected with a caseworker. They will explain what is available and what fits your situation.

Caring for an aging parent or spouse while managing your own life is exhausting. These programs exist specifically for that situation. The call is free and there is no obligation.

SCE CARE & FERA — Discounted Electric Rate — tap for details

Enroll anytime
What it is
Southern California Edison discount programs for income-qualified customers. CARE gives a 32.5% discount on your monthly electric bill. FERA gives an 18% discount.
Who qualifies
Households below income limits based on household size. You may also qualify automatically if you receive Medi-Cal, SSI, CalFresh, or other public assistance.
How to enroll
  • Online: sce.com — search CARE/FERA
  • By phone: (800) 447-6620
  • By voice line: (800) 798-5723

If you already receive Medi-Cal or SSI, you very likely qualify for CARE. Enrollment takes about 10 minutes by phone and the discount applies to your next bill.

SCE also has a one-time Energy Assistance Fund that pays up to $200–$300 directly toward your electric bill. Ask about it when you call.

(800) 447-6620

Property Taxes

California Property Tax Postponement — tap for details

State program
What it is
A California State Controller's Office program that lets eligible homeowners delay payment of current-year property taxes. The deferred amount becomes a lien on your home, repaid when you sell or transfer the property.
Who qualifies
  • Age 62 or older, or blind, or have a disability
  • Own and live in the home
  • At least 40% equity in the home
  • Total annual household income of $55,181 or less
How to apply
Apply directly through the California State Controller's Office. Search "property tax postponement" at sco.ca.gov. Applications are accepted annually.
Why it matters
For a senior on a fixed income who owns their home, property taxes can be the largest recurring bill. This program defers that bill indefinitely so it does not force a sale or create financial hardship.

This program is separate from the property tax exemptions available to veterans. If you or your spouse served in the military, ask the Riverside County Assessor's office about the Veterans' Exemption too.

Apply at sco.ca.gov
