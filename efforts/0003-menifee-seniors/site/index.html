Kay Ceniceros Senior Center Walk-in — tap for details

The city's senior center, run by the City of Menifee's Parks and Community Services department in partnership with the Riverside County Office on Aging. Hot lunches every weekday, fitness and activity programs, and a place to socialize. This is the most accessible senior resource physically in Menifee.

Hot lunch
Served Monday through Friday, 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM. Call at least 24 hours ahead to reserve. A voluntary donation is encouraged to help fund the program. No income requirement.
Activities
Fitness classes, Tai Chi, line dancing, ping pong, cards and board games (bridge, pinochle, dominoes, Texas Hold'em, chess), quilting, and other social clubs. Classes run throughout the week.

YANA: You Are Not Alone Free — tap for details

Run by the Menifee Police Department, YANA is a free check-in program for older adults living alone. Trained volunteers call participants at a regular prearranged time. If there is no answer, volunteers contact family, friends, or neighbors. It exists to give homebound seniors a safety net and give their families peace of mind.

How it works
After enrolling, you receive a phone call from a Menifee PD volunteer at the time you set. You only need to answer the phone. If you do not answer, your emergency contact is reached. No technology, no app, no monthly fee.
Who it is for
Older and disabled adults living alone in the City of Menifee. Family members, neighbors, or caregivers can refer someone on their behalf.
What you need to enroll
  • Ability to answer a phone
  • One emergency contact name and number
How to enroll
Email the YANA coordinator at DG_PD_Volunteers@menifeepolice.org or mail a completed registration form to: Menifee Police Department, ATTN: YANA, 29714 Haun Rd Unit A, Menifee CA 92586. Help completing the form is available by request.
Non-emergency line
(951) 677-4964 — call to ask about YANA or request a form be mailed to you.

If you know someone living alone who has no regular check-in from family or neighbors, this is the right program to tell them about.

In-Home Supportive Services (IHSS) Call to apply — tap for details

IHSS is a California program that helps pay for in-home care for low-income elderly, blind, or disabled people so they can stay safely at home. If you or someone you know needs help with bathing, dressing, cooking, cleaning, or getting to appointments, this program may cover the cost of a paid caregiver.

Who qualifies
Low-income adults 65 or older, blind individuals, or adults with a disability. Must be unable to remain safely at home without in-home services. Riverside County residents including Menifee.
What it covers
Personal care (bathing, dressing, grooming), domestic services (cooking, cleaning, laundry), paramedical services, and protective supervision for adults with cognitive impairments. Services are determined by an in-home assessment.
How to apply
Call the IHSS HOME Call Center at (888) 960-4477, Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM. You can also apply online at riversideihss.org.
Cost
Free to eligible recipients. Program is funded by Medi-Cal and county funds. No cost to the family for the services themselves.

An IHSS social worker will visit the home to assess needs and determine the number of hours authorized. The family can choose their own provider or find one through the county registry.

(888) 960-4477 Apply at riversideihss.org

RTA Dial-A-Ride Call to schedule — tap for details

Dial-A-Ride is a door-to-door transit service for seniors and people with disabilities who cannot use regular bus service. You reserve a ride in advance; a vehicle picks you up at your door and takes you to your destination. Operated by Riverside Transit Agency (RTA).

Who qualifies
Adults 65 or older, or adults with a disability that makes it difficult to use fixed-route bus service. Menifee is within RTA's service area.
Service area
Trips must begin and end within three-quarters of a mile of an RTA fixed-route bus line. Call to confirm your address is in the service area.
How it works
Call in advance to reserve a ride. Specify pickup time and destination. The vehicle comes to your door.
Fares
Reduced fare for seniors and disabled riders. Call or visit the RTA website for current fare information.
How to start
Call the RTA Customer Information Center at (951) 565-5002 to confirm eligibility and learn how to register. Open Monday through Friday.

The TRIP volunteer driver program (listed above) may be more flexible for trips outside the RTA service area, such as medical appointments in other cities.

(951) 565-5002 RTA Dial-A-Ride info

For family caregivers

Riverside County Family Caregiver Support Free — tap for details

Run by the Riverside County Office on Aging under the National Family Caregiver Support Program. Free support for family members caring for an older relative — workshops, support groups, an in-home social worker visit, and respite care when funding allows.

What is included
  • 11-week educational workshop series: dementia, Alzheimer's, caregiver burnout, legal issues, communication
  • Monthly support groups (held at locations throughout Riverside County)
  • In-home assessment with a social worker who helps build a care plan
  • Respite care for the person being cared for (subject to funding availability)
Who qualifies
Family members and informal caregivers caring for an adult 60 or older. No income requirement.
How to access
Call the RC Office on Aging HelpLink at (877) 932-4100, Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. Ask about the Family Caregiver Support Program.

If you are caring for a parent or spouse and feeling overwhelmed, this is the county program designed for exactly that situation. The 11-week workshop and monthly groups give caregivers tools and community.

(877) 932-4100 rcaging.org

Inland Caregiver Resource Center (ICRC) Free — tap for details

ICRC is a state-funded nonprofit that serves family caregivers throughout Riverside, San Bernardino, Inyo, and Mono counties — including Menifee. Particularly strong support for caregivers dealing with Alzheimer's and other dementias.

Services
  • Family consultation — one-on-one help with care planning
  • Short-term counseling for caregiver stress
  • Support groups
  • Educational workshops, including a 4-class series specifically for Alzheimer's and dementia caregivers
  • Respite care coordination
  • Supplemental services based on need
Cost
Free to eligible family caregivers. No income requirements specified.
Hours
Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 5 PM.
How to reach them
Call (800) 675-6694 or email info@inlandcaregivers.org

Utility and financial help

Energy Bill Assistance (LIHEAP / SHARE) Call for appointment — tap for details

LIHEAP (Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program) provides one-time financial help with electric and gas bills for low-income households. Seniors 60 and older receive priority. Administered by Community Action Partnership of Riverside County (CAP).

Who qualifies
Low-income households in Riverside County. Seniors 60+, people with disabilities, and households with children under 6 receive priority.
What it covers
A one-time payment toward your electric or gas bill. Also: the Energy Crisis Intervention Program (ECIP) for weather-related energy emergencies, and weatherization services to reduce future bills.
LIHEAP — appointment required
Call (951) 955-4900 to do a required phone pre-screen, then schedule an appointment.
SHARE — walk-in allowed
The SHARE utility assistance program allows walk-in visits Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. Same number to call or visit in person. Address: CAP offices throughout Riverside County — call for the location nearest Menifee.

If you or someone you know is facing a utility shutoff, call (951) 955-4900 and mention the emergency — there are expedited options for imminent shutoffs.

(951) 955-4900 capriverside.org
