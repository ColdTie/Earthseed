Daily lunch
Monday through Friday, 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM. Reservation required by the previous day. Suggested donation of $3. No one is turned away for inability to pay.

YANA Wellness Check Program Menifee PD — tap for details

YANA (You Are Not Alone) is a free program run by Menifee Police Department volunteers. Participants are called at regular times to make sure they are okay. If someone does not answer, volunteers contact emergency contacts and can make a home visit. A family member or neighbor can refer someone even without the senior's immediate involvement.

Who can enroll
Must live in the City of Menifee, be able to answer a phone, and have one emergency contact person to list.
Cost
Free.
How to enroll
Email DG_PD_Volunteers@menifeepolice.org to request an enrollment form, or mail a completed form to:
Menifee Police Department, ATTN: YANA
29714 Haun Rd, Unit A, Menifee CA 92586
One-time welfare check
If you are concerned about a specific person right now, call the Menifee PD non-emergency line: (951) 677-4964. For an immediate life-threatening situation, call 911.

Family members and neighbors can submit a referral on behalf of a senior. You do not have to wait for the person to enroll themselves.

Vial of Life Riverside County Fire — tap for details

A small kit that keeps your critical medical information — diagnoses, medications, emergency contacts, DNR status — in a clear container on your refrigerator. When first responders enter your home, they know to check the fridge door for it.

How to get a kit
Free. Pick one up at any Menifee fire station during business hours, or at Menifee City Hall. A printable version is also available via QR code at the fire department.
Fire station locations
See cityofmenifee.us/567/Fire-Stations for all Menifee station addresses.
Cost
Free.

Once filled out, place the container on the inside of the refrigerator door and put the Vial of Life sticker on the outside of your front door so first responders know to look for it.

Riverside County Home Delivered Meals Homebound seniors — tap for details

…

If you or someone you know cannot get to a food pantry or the senior center because of age or disability, this program delivers meals to your door. Enrollment starts with a phone call — no office visit required.

Who qualifies
Adults age 60 or older, or adults with a disability, who are homebound and have difficulty preparing their own meals. Serves all of Riverside County including Menifee. No income requirement.
What they provide
Hot or frozen meals delivered to your home. Emergency two-week pantry supply also available.
Call hours
Monday9 AM – 4 PM
Tuesday9 AM – 4 PM
Wednesday9 AM – 4 PM
Thursday9 AM – 4 PM
Friday9 AM – 4 PM
How to start
Call Senior HelpLink at 1-800-510-2020 or (877) 932-4100. Ask about the Home Delivered Meals Program. An intake worker will determine eligibility.

This program exists but is not widely known. If someone cannot leave home to get food, this is the right call to make first.

1-800-510-2020

TRIP Volunteer Driver Program Transportation — tap for details

TRIP (Transportation Reimbursement and Information Project) connects seniors and people with disabilities who can no longer drive with volunteer drivers. The county reimburses riders 45 cents per mile so they can pay their driver. For medical appointments, pharmacy runs, grocery shopping.

Who qualifies
Seniors and adults with disabilities who need rides but cannot drive. Serves all of Riverside County including Menifee.
Cost
No out-of-pocket cost to the rider. The county sends a mileage reimbursement check each month to pay the driver.
How to enroll
Call Independent Living Partnership at (951) 653-0740. Enrollment takes about a month, so call as soon as you think you will need rides.
For volunteer drivers
No training required. Call the same number to sign up as a driver. See the volunteer page for more detail.
(951) 653-0740

Multipurpose Senior Services Program Medi-Cal required — tap for details

MSSP is for frail seniors who are on Medi-Cal and at risk of moving to a nursing home. A care manager coordinates in-home services, medical appointments, and community support to help them stay at home.

Who qualifies
Adults age 65 or older who are on Medi-Cal and have care needs that would otherwise require nursing home care.
What they provide
In-home assessment, care management, help coordinating home health, personal care, transportation, and other services.
How to apply
Call (877) 932-4100 and ask about the Multipurpose Senior Services Program.
(877) 932-4100

Health coverage and caregiver support

HICAP — Free Medicare Counseling Call or visit — tap for details

HICAP (Health Insurance Counseling and Advocacy Program) provides free, unbiased Medicare counseling from trained volunteers. No sales pitch. No conflict of interest. They help you understand your coverage, choose a plan, resolve billing problems, and navigate appeals.

Who can use it
Anyone on Medicare or approaching Medicare enrollment age. Family members can call on behalf of a senior.
Cost
Free.
How to reach them
Call 1-800-434-0222 (statewide line) or 1-800-510-2020 (Riverside County HelpLink). When you call, ask specifically about HICAP counseling and whether any appointments are available in or near Menifee.
Nearest confirmed site
Mary Phillips Senior Center, 41845 Sixth Street, Temecula, CA 92590. Appointments available there.

HICAP counseling may be available at the Kay Ceniceros Senior Center in Menifee. Call 1-800-510-2020 to ask specifically about Menifee-area appointments before driving to Temecula.

1-800-434-0222

Family Caregiver Support Program RC Office on Aging — tap for details

For family members, friends, or neighbors who are caring for an older adult or a person with dementia. Caregiving is exhausting. This program offers free education, support groups, and professional guidance — no income requirement.

Who can use it
Anyone acting as an unpaid caregiver for an adult age 60 or older, or for a person of any age with Alzheimer's or dementia. Grandparents raising grandchildren (age 55+) also qualify.
What they provide
  • 11-week educational workshop series (dementia, burnout, legal issues, communication)
  • Monthly support groups, county-wide
  • In-home social worker assessment for care planning
  • Respite care referrals for enrolled families
Cost
Free. No income requirement.
How to enroll
Call (877) 932-4100 or 1-800-510-2020 and ask about the Family Caregiver Support Program.
(877) 932-4100 rcaging.org

Adult Protective Services Elder abuse and neglect — tap for details

If you suspect that an older adult is being abused, neglected, or is neglecting themselves, APS is the right call. Reports can be made anonymously. APS investigators are separate from police and approach the situation as social workers, not law enforcement.

Who to call
Anyone who suspects elder abuse, financial exploitation, physical abuse, neglect, or self-neglect of an older adult or dependent adult in Riverside County.
Hotline
(800) 491-7123 — available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
Nearest DPSS office
43264 Business Park Drive, Suite 102, Temecula CA 92590 — (951) 600-6500

Reports can be made anonymously. You do not need proof — if something feels wrong, call and let the investigators assess.

(800) 491-7123

Free Legal Help for Seniors ICLS — tap for details

Inland Counties Legal Services (ICLS) gives free legal help to seniors in Riverside County who cannot afford a lawyer. They serve Menifee ZIP codes and do outreach for seniors who cannot travel to their Riverside office.

Who can use it
Seniors in Riverside County, particularly those in greatest economic or social need. Income limits apply for some services.
What they help with
Wills, Powers of Attorney, small estate probate, nursing home issues, housing law, mobile home law, elder abuse cases, grandparent visitation, health care law, public benefits, consumer issues, small claims.
Phone
(888) 245-4257 — Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:45 PM
Office
1040 Iowa Ave, Unit 109, Riverside CA 92507 (outreach available for those who cannot travel)
(888) 245-4257 inlandlegal.org

Utility bill help

Utility Assistance Programs — tap for details

Several programs lower or cover utility bills for income-qualified households in Menifee. Many seniors qualify for more than one. The programs below do not all require applying at the same place.

ELECTRICITY & GAS — INCOME BASED

LIHEAP
One-time payment on your electric or gas bill. Federal program, local application.
CAP Riverside: (951) 955-4900
SCE CARE
30–35% off your SCE electric bill. Auto-qualify if on Medi-Cal, CalFresh, or SSI.
(800) 798-5723 · sce.com
SCE FERA
18% off electric bill for households of 3+ people just above CARE income limits.
(800) 798-5723 · sce.com
SoCalGas CARE
20% off your monthly gas bill. Same income qualifications as SCE CARE.
socalgas.com or (800) 427-2200

ELECTRICITY — MEDICAL NEED (NO INCOME REQUIREMENT)

SCE Medical Baseline
Extra electricity at the lowest rate for life-support equipment, temperature-sensitive conditions, or life-threatening illness. No income limit.
(800) 447-6620

GAS — ONE-TIME EMERGENCY HELP

SoCalGas Gas Assistance Fund
One-time grant up to $100; up to $200 if any household member is 55 or older. Applied through local nonprofit partners. Limited funds — apply early in the program year.
(800) 331-7593 to find a local agency

To apply for LIHEAP, call CAP Riverside at (951) 955-4900 — a phone pre-screen is required before scheduling an appointment. For SCE programs, apply at sce.com or call (800) 798-5723. For SoCalGas programs, apply at socalgas.com.
