Getting Around
TRIP Volunteer Driver Program
Free Program
Riverside County Independent Living Partnership

TRIP lets you use a volunteer driver (a friend, neighbor, or someone you recruit) to get to medical appointments, the pharmacy, the grocery store, or other errands. The county reimburses you 45 cents per mile each month, and you pay your driver from that. No special training is required to drive.

  • Who can useSeniors and people with disabilities who cannot drive or use public transit. Must be a Riverside County resident.
  • Your driverYou choose someone you already know. If you do not have a driver, ILP can sometimes help connect you.
  • Trips coveredMedical appointments, pharmacies, grocery stores, senior centers, and other essential destinations.
  • CostFree to enroll. You receive a monthly reimbursement check to pay your driver back for mileage.
Steve Penner is a volunteer driver available through this program for Menifee-area seniors. Call ILP at (951) 653-0740 to be matched.

GoGo Grandparent — On-Demand Rides
City Partnership
City of Menifee program Seniors 60+ and adults with disabilities

GoGo Grandparent arranges Uber or Lyft rides for you without needing a smartphone. Call an operator any time, day or night, and they handle it. The City of Menifee gives qualified residents their first four one-way rides free.

  • Who can useMenifee residents age 60 or older, or adults with disabilities.
  • First 4 ridesFree through the City of Menifee partnership.
  • After thatYou pay the ride cost directly (Uber/Lyft rates).
  • How to registerCall (951) 556-6625 or visit the Kay Ceniceros Senior Center in person.
  • Available24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
📞 Register: (951) 556-6625 gogograndparent.com/menifee

Riverside County Home Delivered Meals
Call to Enroll
All of Riverside County including Menifee For homebound seniors

The county delivers meals to your home if you are 60 or older and unable to leave easily due to illness or disability. One phone call starts the intake process — no office visit required. This program is not well-advertised locally; many people who need it do not know it exists.

  • Who can useAdults 60 or older who are homebound and cannot prepare their own meals. Also covers adults with disabilities.
  • Income limitNone stated; priority given to greatest nutritional need.
  • What you getHot or frozen meals delivered to your door, a two-week emergency food supply, and nutrition resources.
  • Call hoursMonday–Friday, 9 AM – 4 PM
📞 Call: (877) 932-4100 RC Office on Aging
Safety and Staying Connected
YANA — You Are Not Alone (Menifee PD)
Free
Menifee Police Department Volunteer Program

Menifee PD volunteers call you at a prearranged time to check in. If you do not pick up, they contact your emergency contact and if needed, send someone to check on you in person. Free. No law enforcement record is created — this is a care program, not an enforcement one.

  • Who can useOlder adults and adults with disabilities living alone in Menifee.
  • How to joinEmail the program coordinator. They will send a registration form. You set the call schedule.
  • RequirementsMust be able to answer the phone. Must provide one emergency contact.
  • CostCompletely free.

For checking on a neighbor or family member

In-Home Support and Caregiver Help
In-Home Supportive Services (IHSS)
Apply by Phone
Riverside County DPSS For seniors and people with disabilities

IHSS pays a caregiver to help you with daily tasks at home — often a family member can be your paid caregiver. The goal is to let you stay in your own home safely and independently for as long as possible.

  • ServicesMeal prep, food shopping, bathing, dressing, personal care, housecleaning, medication assistance.
  • Who qualifiesSeniors and adults with disabilities who need help with daily tasks to stay safely at home.
  • Your caregiverCan be a family member. IHSS will also help match you with a qualified provider if needed.
  • CostNo direct cost to the recipient if you qualify.
📞 Call: (888) 960-4477 Learn more
Family Caregiver Support Program
Free
Riverside County Office on Aging

A free 11-week workshop series for family members who are caring for an older adult. Covers managing caregiving challenges, navigating county services, and preventing caregiver burnout. Workshops held at various locations throughout Riverside County.

  • Who it is forAnyone caring for a family member age 60 or older.
  • FormatGroup workshops, approximately 11 sessions.
  • Respite careMay be available to give caregivers a break. Ask when you call.

Financial and Home Help
Senior Home Repair Grant (MHRGP)
City of Menifee
Up to $15,000 — one-time lifetime grant Low-income senior homeowners

The City of Menifee offers a home repair grant specifically for low-income seniors and permanently disabled homeowners. The grant pays for safety and accessibility improvements — things like ramp installation, bathroom grab bars, roof and plumbing repairs, and code violations.

  • Who qualifiesSeniors or permanently disabled persons who own and live in their home. Must have owned and lived there for at least one year.
  • Income limitGross household income at or below 50% of Riverside County Area Median Income.
  • Grant amountUp to $15,000 lifetime. This is a grant, not a loan.
  • What it coversAccessibility (ramps, grab bars, wider doors), safety (roof, electrical, plumbing), code violations.
  • ContactCelia Olivas, City of Menifee
📞 Call: (951) 723-1750 Program page
Utility Bill Assistance (LIHEAP/HEAP)
Call for Appointment
Community Action Partnership of Riverside County

Federal funding helps pay heating, cooling, water, sewer, trash, phone, and internet bills for low-income households in Riverside County. Apply by calling first for a phone pre-screen, then schedule an appointment.

  • Who qualifiesHousehold income below 60% of the State Median Income. Seniors with fixed incomes often qualify.
  • What it coversElectric, gas, water, sewer, trash, phone, internet.
  • Step 1Call (951) 955-4900 for a phone pre-screen before scheduling.
  • Crisis helpIf your utilities are about to be disconnected, mention that when you call — emergency assistance may be available faster.
📞 Call: (951) 955-4900 CAP Riverside
