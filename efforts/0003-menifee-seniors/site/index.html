Thursday9 AM – 4 PM

Hot or frozen meals. An emergency two-week pantry supply is also available.

Getting to appointments

TRIP Volunteer Driver Program — tap for details

Call to enroll

TRIP connects seniors and people with disabilities who can no longer drive with community volunteers who give rides. The county reimburses you 45 cents per mile so you can pay the driver back. You are not dependent on the driver's goodwill — the reimbursement makes it a fair exchange.

What it covers
Doctor appointments, pharmacy, grocery store, errands. Trips you arrange in advance with your driver.
How it works
You are matched with a volunteer driver. The county sends you a monthly mileage reimbursement check. You pay the driver back from that check at 45¢/mile.
To enroll
Call Independent Living Partnership at (951) 653-0740. Enrollment takes approximately one month. If you have someone in mind who can drive you, they can register as your named driver.
Service area
All of Riverside County, including Menifee.

Enrollment takes about a month. Call before you need the rides, not during a crisis.

(951) 653-0740 Volunteer driver info

In-home help

In-Home Supportive Services (IHSS) Medi-Cal required — tap for details

Phone & online

IHSS pays for a caregiver to come to your home and help with daily tasks. It is free for people who qualify. The goal is to keep you living safely in your own home rather than moving to a facility.

Who qualifies
Adults 65 or older, blind, or disabled who are eligible for Medi-Cal or receiving SSI. You must need help with daily tasks to qualify — an in-home assessment is part of the process.
What it covers
Housecleaning, meal preparation, laundry, grocery shopping, personal care (bathing, grooming), accompaniment to medical appointments, medication reminders.
How to apply
Apply online at RiversideIHSS.org or call the HOME Call Center at (888) 960-4477. Phone applications are answered Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM.
Cost
Free for eligible participants. IHSS pays the caregiver directly.

A family member or trusted friend can be your paid caregiver through IHSS. They must go through the enrollment process. Call to ask how that works.

(888) 960-4477 Apply online

Wellness and safety

YANA — You Are Not Alone — tap for details

Free program

A free Menifee Police program run by trained volunteers. They call you at a prearranged time on a regular schedule. If you do not answer, they contact your emergency contact, then neighbors, then check on you in person if needed. For seniors and disabled adults living alone.

What it is
Regular scheduled phone calls from a Menifee PD volunteer. If you miss a call, your emergency contact and neighbors are notified. The volunteer comes to your door if no one can reach you.
Requirements
  • Able to answer the phone at a scheduled time
  • Provide one emergency contact
Cost
Free. Run by Menifee PD volunteers.
How to register
Email the YANA coordinator at DG_PD_Volunteers@menifeepolice.org or return a completed application to:
Menifee Police Department, ATTN: YANA
29714 Haun Rd, Unit A, Menifee, CA 92586
More info
menifeepolice.org/youarenotalone

This is a real program run by Menifee's own police department. If you live alone and have family members who worry, this is a good call to make.

Financial help

Utility Bill Assistance (LIHEAP / HEAP) — tap for details

By appointment

The Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program helps pay electric and gas bills. Seniors 60 and older are a priority group. Weatherization help (attic insulation, weather stripping, minor repairs) is also available to lower energy costs long-term.

Who qualifies
Households at or below 150% of the federal poverty level. Seniors 60+, people with disabilities, and households with children under 6 receive priority.
What it covers
One-time payment toward an electric or gas bill. Emergency help available for crisis situations. Weatherization services to reduce future bills.
How to apply
Call Community Action Partnership of Riverside County at (951) 955-4900. A phone pre-screen is required before scheduling an appointment. Emergency walk-in hours also available.

Applications are accepted by appointment. Call first — do not arrive at the office without calling.

(951) 955-4900 CAP Riverside details

Minor Home Repair Grant — City of Menifee — tap for details

Up to $10,000

The City of Menifee offers grants of up to $10,000 to eligible senior citizens for home repairs. This is a city program, not a loan — it does not have to be repaid if eligibility requirements are met.

What it covers
Minor home repairs. Specific eligible repairs and income limits are set by the city. Call to confirm current rules and whether you qualify.
How to find out
Contact the City of Menifee's Parks and Community Services or the city main line and ask about the Minor Home Rehabilitation Grant Program for seniors.
Contact
(951) 672-6777 or cityofmenifee.us/831

Confirm current availability when you call. Grant programs can have funding limits and waiting lists.

(951) 672-6777

For family caregivers

Family Caregiver Support Program — tap for details

Free

Caring for an older family member is hard. Riverside County Office on Aging runs a free program specifically for family caregivers. No income limit. Available throughout the county, including Menifee.

Who it is for
Anyone caring for a family member age 60 or older, or caring for an adult with Alzheimer's or a related dementia (regardless of age).
What they provide
  • One-on-one counseling and case management
  • Respite care — temporary relief so you can take a break
  • Support groups
  • Care Pathways: free 11-week educational workshop on caregiving topics
  • Help finding other services
Cost
Free. No income requirement for most services.
How to access
Call the HelpLink line at (877) 932-4100 and ask about the Family Caregiver Support Program.

Caregiver burnout is a real risk. Respite is part of the program, not an afterthought. Ask about it specifically when you call.

(877) 932-4100 rcaging.org
