# Earthseed

The name comes from Octavia Butler's *Parable of the Sower*. In that book, Lauren Olamina builds a belief system called Earthseed around one core truth: God is Change. You cannot stop what changes. You can only shape it.

That is what this project tries to do. Not stop problems. Shape the small part of the world that is reachable from here.

## The honest version of what this is

A person cannot fix hunger or housing or loneliness at scale from a laptop. But a specific person in Menifee, California who does not know where to get food this week can be helped by a web page that has the right address and the right hours. That is not nothing. That is real.

The aim is to find problems like that, small and specific and solvable, and build the thing that solves them. Then check whether it actually solved them. Then fix what did not work. Then find the next one.

There is no grand plan past that loop. The loop is the plan.

## What scales and what does not

Attention does not scale well. Money scales better. Over time the goal is for this work to generate something that can be given away, and to give it away with the same care and specificity as everything else here: not in bulk, not vaguely, but to real people and real causes with clear reasoning for each.

That part is in the future. The work right now is proving the loop works on small problems first.

## What this is not

It is not a startup. It is not a nonprofit yet. It is not a platform. It is not content. It is not about the technology.

It is an honest attempt to help specific people, measured by whether they were actually helped, operated by a person and an agent working together inside clear rules.

That is the whole thing.
