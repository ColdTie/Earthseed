# Earthseed

The name comes from Octavia Butler's *Parable of the Sower*. In that book, Lauren Olamina builds a belief system called Earthseed around one core truth: God is Change. You cannot stop what changes. You can only shape it.

That is what this project tries to do. Not stop problems. Shape the small part of the world that is reachable from here.

## The two problems this project works on

**Problem one: people cannot find the help that exists.**
A person in Menifee who needs food, a ride to the doctor, or a utility bill paid does not know where to go. The resources exist. The information about them is buried, scattered, and often wrong. A verified, maintained, plain-language web page fixes this for a specific person on a specific day. That is real and measurable.

**Problem two: people vote against their own interests because they cannot see the connection.**
A family losing SNAP benefits, a senior losing Medi-Cal coverage, a renter being priced out — these are not just bad luck. They are the downstream result of specific votes cast by specific people. Most people never make that connection because translating "bill passed in Washington" into "here is what that means for your paycheck and your family" requires work nobody does for them.

VoterImpact does that work. You enter your zip code, your income range, and your honest answers to a few policy questions. The tool shows you which programs you qualify for, how your representatives actually voted on legislation that affects those programs, and where their record aligns or conflicts with your stated interests. No recommendations. No telling you who to vote for. Just: here is what happened, here is who did it, here is how it affects you. You decide.

These two problems are connected. The resource pages treat the immediate need. VoterImpact addresses the upstream cause. Both matter and both belong here.

## Why Menifee first

A person cannot fix politics at scale from a laptop. But Menifee is a real place with real people, real representatives, and real policies affecting them. Building something that works for one specific community — accurately, verifiably, usefully — is the proof that the approach works. Then it scales.

## What scales and what does not

Attention does not scale well. Money scales better. The aim is for this work to generate revenue through grants, through selling the directory methodology to organizations that need it, and eventually through VoterImpact reaching enough people through social media that it sustains itself. The giving model — donating a portion of revenue to real people and causes — is part of the long-term picture once the legal and financial infrastructure exists.

## What this is not

It is not a political campaign. It is not a nonprofit yet. It is not a media company. It is not content for its own sake.

It is a civic tool and a community resource, built to be honest about what it is, measured on whether it actually helps people, and improved based on what the data says.

That is the whole thing.
